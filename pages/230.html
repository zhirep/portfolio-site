Slimy Ascent

Project Summary

Slimy Ascent is a 2D platformer where the player must control the slime and guide it through challenging levels to safety. The project was developed in Unity using the programming language C#. The textures and sprites used were drawn in Asesprite. This was a project for an introductory gaming course and was developed in a span of around 8 weeks and was completed with 3 other people.

Problem and Goal

In Slimy Ascent, you play as Slimey. With your companion Gui, your goal is to survive a dangerous world by ascending to great heights.

My Role

My roal was a mix of UI and texture designer and game logic developer. For UI, I developed the entire visual of the game for the textures,animations and assets using an app called Asesprite.

As a developer, I developed core functions for the wall sliding and hazard logic. In addition to development, I also did some testing to ensure that the game flows naturally and fix if any bugs occur.

Technologies Used

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Asesprite
