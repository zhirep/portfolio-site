Comtutor

Project Summary

Comtutor is a project I worked on for my Software Engineering course. It is a mobile application specifically made for students to browse a collection of tutors. It has a built-in filter with a variety of options to customize to match a student with a qualified tutor based on their subject and grade level. The app was built and designed using Android Studio using the Kotlin programming language. The project used XML to display the screens and Firebase for its database. This was a project done in around 12 weeks and was completed with 4 other people.

Problem and Goal

Students who are struggling academically may not have the right tools they need to get the help that they need. With Comtutor, we provide them with a solution by offering them a mobile application that lets them connect to qualified tutors with ease, based on their academic needs.

My Role

My role in Comtutor was primarily as a UI frontend developer. I designed and developed the majority of the screen pages for the app, such as the Login and Register pages, the Homepage, Scheduling and Booking pages, Messaging page, and many more! As the frontend developer, I had to ensure that the pages were functional, intuitive and easy to use, and more importantly, consistent with our goal to give students a pleasant user experience.

In addition to being a frontend developer, I worked together with the backend developers to display information from the database for general user interface to show vital information to the users, like their schedule, messaging chats, and personal information. I also worked on testing, ensuring that the database information translates well as UI.

Video Transcript

Sprint 1

  • The user is greeted by the login page, where they can input their credentials, such as their email and password. Additionally, there is a toggle that indicates if the user is a student or a tutor. Optionally, toggling the "Remember me" box will ensure that the user remains signed in when booting up the app again.
  • If the user doesn't have an account yet, they may click on the register button to register a new account. In the register page, the user is asked to enter their email and password, as well as indicate if the user is a student or a tutor. Alternatively, the user may register using their Google account.
  • Upon logging in/registration, the user is redirected to the home page. At the time of recording, the page was only a placeholder, and the only functional button was the sign-out.
  • The video also demonstrates the data contained in the database.

Sprint 2

Student View
  • In this sprint demo, the steps mostly remained the same. The main difference was in the homepage, where the hamburger menu with all the shortcuts to pages, is implemented.
  • Clicking on the "Book a Lesson" button redirects the user to the booking page. Here, the user may customize their booking through the course, grade, session length, and a date.
  • Clicking "Next page" will load the available tutors based on the user's booking settings. Here, details about tutors such as their name, the course they teach, and their availability is displayed. If no tutors are matched, a message displays "No Tutors Matched."
  • Clicking "Pick" prompts the user to select a timeslot. Upon completion, the user is redirected back to the home page.
  • Going to the Schedule page will display a calendar view. Clicking on a date with a scheduled appointment will display details about the session.
Tutor View
  • The hamburger menu in a tutor account is slightly different, with an added "Availability" button.
  • Navigating to the Schedule page and clicking on a date with an appointment scheduled will display booking details.

Sprint 3

Student View
  • In this sprint, the homepage is finalized with more button functionalities.
  • User flow is mainly the same, with differences on pages like the Booking and Schedule pages, where it now highlights dates in the calendar green if there is a scheduled appointment on the day. Additionally, upon successfully booking an appointment, the system sends the student a confirmation email of the booking.
  • Navigating to the Modify Session page lets the user modify or cancel an existing appointment.
  • Clicking the message button will redirect the user to the Messaging page. On this page, the student may look for a professor and send messages to them. Optionally, they may also upload file documents for the recipients to view.
Tutor View
  • Clicking on the "View Profile" will display details about the tutor, such as their name, the courses they are qualified to teach, their email contact, their profile age, and their tutor status. Tutor status is set to "Active" only if an admin verifies the tutor. On the same page, tutors may also add more courses and its corresponding grades to courses they are qualified to teach.
  • Going to the Document Upload page will let them upload documents such as their certificates that ensure they are qualified to teach, which an admin of the page can check and verify.
  • On the availability page, the tutor may set their availability dates and times
Admin View
  • As an admin, they may view documents uploaded by tutors and verify them. Upon verification, they may approve or reject tutor applications.
