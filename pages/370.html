Crossy Roads 3D

Project Summary

Crossy Roads 3D is a top down platformer where the goal is get as far as possible without getting hit by the moving traffic. The project was developed in WebGL and assets were built from an in-house engine. This was a project done in around 8 weeks and was completed with another person.

Problem and Goal

You play as the ladybug trying to get to safety and in order to do so, you will have to cross the road without getting hit by the moving hazards.

My Role

My role in Crossy Roads 3D was a main developer. I worked on implementing the logic for the lighting and shader, ensuring that the game scene is visible and rendered correctly.

I also worked on implementing the side objective of the game which is the coin collection. Features like UI displays and SFX implementation were also developed by me as well.

Video Transcript

  • You, as the player, play a ladybug trying to cross to safety.
  • The player demonstrates the controls and the collision of the walls.
  • Coins can be collected, and the amount is displayed on the bottom left of the screen.
  • Pressing space switches the perspective from top down to first-person view. Additionally, you can see the lanes get generated as the player moves forward.
  • Colliding with a vehicle plays an SFX and displays the game-over screen.
